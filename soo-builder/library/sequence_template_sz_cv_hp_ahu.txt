SINGLE ZONE CV HEAT PUMP AHU SEQUENCE OF OPERATIONS
TYPICAL FOR AHU-1, AHU-2, AHU-3

AHU CV - HEAT PUMP WITH HOT GAS REHEAT AND ELECTRIC AUX. HEAT - SINGLE ZONE - SEQUENCE OF OPERATIONS

GENERAL
1. THIS SEQUENCE APPLIES TO A CONSTANT VOLUME (CV), SINGLE-ZONE AIR HANDLING UNIT (AHU) SERVING ONE THERMAL ZONE. THE AHU IS PAIRED WITH A SPLIT-SYSTEM HEAT PUMP CONDENSING UNIT (CU) PROVIDING DIRECT EXPANSION (DX) COOLING, HEAT PUMP HEATING, AND HOT GAS REHEAT (HGRH). THE HGRH COIL IS LOCATED DOWNSTREAM OF THE DX/HP COIL. AUXILIARY ELECTRIC HEAT IS PROVIDED BY A CONTACTOR-STAGED ELECTRIC HEATER LOCATED DOWNSTREAM OF THE SUPPLY FAN.
2. AHU-1 AND AHU-2: SINGLE-STAGE COMPRESSOR AND SINGLE-STAGE ELECTRIC HEAT. AHU-3: TWO-STAGE COMPRESSOR AND TWO-STAGE ELECTRIC HEAT.
3. STAGING (AHU-3): STAGE 1 SHALL ENERGIZE ON A CALL. STAGE 2 SHALL ENERGIZE WHEN THE CONTROLLED VARIABLE REMAINS 1.5°F (ADJ.) FROM SETPOINT (OR ABOVE HUMIDITY SETPOINT) FOR 10 MINUTES (ADJ.) WITH STAGE 1 RUNNING. STAGES SHALL DE-ENERGIZE IN REVERSE ORDER. INTERSTAGE DELAY SHALL BE 3 MINUTES (ADJ.) FOR COMPRESSORS AND 1 MINUTE (ADJ.) FOR ELECTRIC HEAT. AHU-1 AND AHU-2 SHALL CYCLE THE SINGLE STAGE.
4. OUTSIDE AIR IS SET BY A MANUAL VOLUME DAMPER (MVD), SET DURING TEST AND BALANCE. OUTSIDE AIR IS INTRODUCED WHENEVER THE SUPPLY FAN RUNS. AN OUTSIDE AIRFLOW MEASURING STATION IS PROVIDED FOR MONITORING ONLY.
5. DEFROST SHALL BE CONTROLLED BY THE CU FACTORY CONTROLLER.

CONTROL SYSTEM
1. THE UNIT SHALL BE CONTROLLED BY THE BUILDING AUTOMATION SYSTEM (BAS) THROUGH A DEDICATED DIRECT DIGITAL CONTROL (DDC) CONTROLLER INTERFACING WITH THE CU FACTORY-PACKAGED CONTROLLER.
2. THE BAS SHALL MONITOR AND TREND ALL POINTS LISTED ON THE DDC POINTS LIST.
3. ALL SETPOINTS AND TIME DELAYS LISTED HEREIN ARE ADJUSTABLE (ADJ.) THROUGH THE BAS UNLESS NOTED OTHERWISE.

OPERATING MODES
1. THE UNIT SHALL OPERATE IN OCCUPIED MODE OR UNOCCUPIED MODE AS DETERMINED BY THE BAS SCHEDULE OR LOCAL TENANT OVERRIDE. WITHIN EACH MODE THE UNIT SHALL OPERATE IN COOLING, HEATING, DEHUMIDIFICATION, OR FAN ONLY.
2. LOCAL TENANT OVERRIDE SHALL PLACE THE UNIT IN OCCUPIED MODE FOR 2 HOURS (ADJ.), THEN RETURN TO SCHEDULE.
3. COOLING AND HEAT PUMP HEATING SHALL NOT OPERATE AT THE SAME TIME. A CHANGEOVER DELAY OF 5 MINUTES (ADJ.) SHALL BE ENFORCED BETWEEN COOLING AND HEATING.

STARTUP SEQUENCE
1. UPON A CALL TO ENTER OCCUPIED MODE, THE BAS SHALL VERIFY NO ACTIVE UNIT LOCKOUTS (SMOKE DETECTOR, HIGH STATIC, ELECTRIC HEATER HIGH-LIMIT, EMERGENCY HVAC SHUTDOWN) EXIST. A COMPRESSOR FAULT OR OUTSIDE AIR TEMPERATURE LOCKOUT SHALL BLOCK THE AFFECTED FUNCTION ONLY AND SHALL NOT PREVENT THE SUPPLY FAN FROM STARTING.
2. THE SUPPLY FAN SHALL START. UPON PROOF OF AIRFLOW, THE BAS SHALL ENABLE COMPRESSOR, HGRH, AND ELECTRIC HEAT CONTROL.
3. IF PROOF OF AIRFLOW IS NOT ESTABLISHED WITHIN 60 SECONDS (ADJ.) OF THE FAN START COMMAND, THE BAS SHALL SHUT DOWN THE UNIT AND GENERATE A SUPPLY FAN FAILURE ALARM.

OCCUPIED MODE
1. THE SUPPLY FAN SHALL RUN CONTINUOUSLY.
2. OCCUPIED COOLING SETPOINT SHALL BE 75°F (ADJ.). OCCUPIED HEATING SETPOINT SHALL BE 68°F (ADJ.). THE BAS SHALL ENFORCE A MINIMUM DEADBAND OF 2°F (ADJ.) BETWEEN SETPOINTS.
3. WHEN ZONE TEMPERATURE IS BETWEEN SETPOINTS AND NO DEHUMIDIFICATION CALL EXISTS, THE UNIT SHALL OPERATE IN FAN ONLY.

COMPRESSOR CONTROL
1. COOLING: ON A RISE IN ZONE TEMPERATURE ABOVE THE ACTIVE COOLING SETPOINT, THE BAS SHALL POSITION THE REVERSING VALVE TO COOLING AND STAGE THE COMPRESSOR. THE COMPRESSOR SHALL DE-ENERGIZE WHEN ZONE TEMPERATURE FALLS 1°F (ADJ.) BELOW THE COOLING SETPOINT.
2. HEATING: ON A DROP IN ZONE TEMPERATURE BELOW THE ACTIVE HEATING SETPOINT, THE BAS SHALL POSITION THE REVERSING VALVE TO HEATING AND STAGE THE COMPRESSOR. THE COMPRESSOR SHALL DE-ENERGIZE WHEN ZONE TEMPERATURE RISES 1°F (ADJ.) ABOVE THE HEATING SETPOINT. THE HEAT PUMP SHALL BE THE PRIMARY HEATING SOURCE.
3. MINIMUM COMPRESSOR ON-TIME SHALL BE 3 MINUTES (ADJ.). MINIMUM COMPRESSOR OFF-TIME SHALL BE 5 MINUTES (ADJ.).
4. COIL LOW LIMIT (COOLING AND DEHUMIDIFICATION ONLY): IF DX/HP COIL LEAVING AIR TEMPERATURE FALLS BELOW 45°F (ADJ.), THE BAS SHALL DE-ENERGIZE ONE COMPRESSOR STAGE UNTIL LEAVING AIR TEMPERATURE RISES ABOVE 50°F (ADJ.).
5. LOCKOUTS: COOLING SHALL BE LOCKED OUT BELOW A LOW-AMBIENT SETPOINT OF 20°F (ADJ.) OUTSIDE AIR TEMPERATURE. HEAT PUMP HEATING SHALL BE LOCKED OUT BELOW 20°F (ADJ.) OUTSIDE AIR TEMPERATURE OR UPON A COMPRESSOR FAULT. LOCKOUTS SHALL BE LOGGED AS EVENTS, NOT ALARMS.

AUXILIARY ELECTRIC HEAT CONTROL
1. THE ELECTRIC HEATER SHALL OPERATE AS AUXILIARY HEAT ONLY AND SHALL NOT BE USED FOR DEHUMIDIFICATION REHEAT.
2. AUXILIARY ELECTRIC HEAT SHALL BE ENABLED WHEN ANY ONE OF THE FOLLOWING CONDITIONS IS MET:
   A. THE HEAT PUMP IS AT FULL CAPACITY AND ZONE TEMPERATURE REMAINS MORE THAN 1.5°F (ADJ.) BELOW THE ACTIVE HEATING SETPOINT FOR 10 MINUTES (ADJ.), OR
   B. HEAT PUMP HEATING IS LOCKED OUT, OR
   C. THE CU IS IN DEFROST WHILE THE UNIT IS IN HEATING, TO TEMPER THE SUPPLY AIR.
3. ELECTRIC HEAT SHALL STAGE TO MAINTAIN THE HEATING SETPOINT AND DE-ENERGIZE WHEN ZONE TEMPERATURE RISES TO WITHIN 0.5°F (ADJ.) OF THE HEATING SETPOINT. THE HEAT PUMP SHALL REMAIN ENERGIZED.
4. THE BAS SHALL DE-ENERGIZE ONE STAGE OF ELECTRIC HEAT IF DISCHARGE AIR TEMPERATURE EXCEEDS 95°F (ADJ.).

DEHUMIDIFICATION CONTROL
1. OCCUPIED MODE: WHEN RETURN AIR RELATIVE HUMIDITY RISES ABOVE 60% RH (ADJ.), THE BAS SHALL INDEX THE UNIT TO DEHUMIDIFICATION. DEHUMIDIFICATION SHALL END WHEN RETURN AIR RELATIVE HUMIDITY FALLS BELOW 55% RH (ADJ.).
2. IN DEHUMIDIFICATION, THE BAS SHALL POSITION THE REVERSING VALVE TO COOLING AND STAGE THE COMPRESSOR ON RETURN AIR HUMIDITY, SUBJECT TO THE COMPRESSOR TIMERS, COIL LOW LIMIT, AND COOLING LOCKOUT.
3. THE BAS SHALL ENABLE HGRH TO MAINTAIN ZONE TEMPERATURE AT THE MIDPOINT BETWEEN THE ACTIVE HEATING AND COOLING SETPOINTS (ADJ.). HGRH SHALL OPERATE ONLY WITH THE REVERSING VALVE IN COOLING AND THE COMPRESSOR RUNNING.
4. IF ZONE TEMPERATURE RISES ABOVE THE ACTIVE COOLING SETPOINT, THE BAS SHALL DISABLE HGRH AND OPERATE IN COOLING UNTIL SATISFIED, THEN RETURN TO DEHUMIDIFICATION IF THE CALL REMAINS.
5. IF ZONE TEMPERATURE FALLS BELOW THE ACTIVE HEATING SETPOINT WITH HGRH ACTIVE, DEHUMIDIFICATION SHALL BE SUSPENDED AND THE UNIT SHALL TRANSITION TO HEAT PUMP HEATING AFTER THE CHANGEOVER DELAY.

UNOCCUPIED MODE
1. THE SUPPLY FAN SHALL BE OFF EXCEPT AS FOLLOWS.
2. UNOCCUPIED COOLING SETPOINT SHALL BE 80°F (ADJ.). UNOCCUPIED HEATING SETPOINT SHALL BE 65°F (ADJ.).
3. ON A CALL FOR COOLING OR HEATING AT THE UNOCCUPIED SETPOINTS, THE BAS SHALL START THE SUPPLY FAN AND, UPON PROOF OF FLOW, OPERATE AS DESCRIBED ABOVE UNTIL THE ZONE IS 2°F (ADJ.) INSIDE THE UNOCCUPIED SETPOINT, THEN RETURN TO OFF.
4. UNOCCUPIED DEHUMIDIFICATION: WHEN RETURN AIR RELATIVE HUMIDITY RISES ABOVE 65% RH (ADJ.), THE BAS SHALL START THE SUPPLY FAN AND OPERATE IN DEHUMIDIFICATION USING THE UNOCCUPIED SETPOINTS UNTIL RETURN AIR RELATIVE HUMIDITY FALLS BELOW 60% RH (ADJ.).

SHUTDOWN SEQUENCE
1. UPON A CALL TO ENTER UNOCCUPIED MODE OR A MANUAL STOP COMMAND, THE BAS SHALL DE-ENERGIZE THE COMPRESSOR, HGRH, AND ELECTRIC HEAT.
2. THE SUPPLY FAN SHALL CONTINUE RUNNING FOR A 60-SECOND (ADJ.) PURGE PERIOD AFTER THE ELECTRIC HEAT IS DE-ENERGIZED, THEN STOP.

SAFETIES AND INTERLOCKS
1. UNIT SHUTDOWN: THE FOLLOWING SHALL STOP THE SUPPLY FAN AND DE-ENERGIZE THE COMPRESSOR, HGRH, AND ELECTRIC HEAT THROUGH A HARDWIRED INTERLOCK INDEPENDENT OF THE BAS. EACH SHALL REQUIRE MANUAL RESET.
   A. DUCT SMOKE DETECTOR (WHERE PROVIDED), CONNECTED TO THE FIRE ALARM SYSTEM.
   B. EMERGENCY HVAC SHUTDOWN SWITCH (ESDS).
   C. HIGH STATIC PRESSURE SWITCH, ON DUCT STATIC PRESSURE ABOVE THE DUCTWORK DESIGN LIMIT.
2. UPON ESDS ACTIVATION, THE LOW-LEAKAGE MOTORIZED DAMPERS AT THE BUILDING OUTSIDE AIR INTAKE AND EXHAUST LOUVERS SHALL CLOSE THROUGH A HARDWIRED INTERLOCK AND REMAIN CLOSED UNTIL THE SWITCH IS RESET. THE DAMPERS SHALL REMAIN OPEN AT ALL OTHER TIMES.
3. THE ELECTRIC HEATER SHALL BE HARDWIRE INTERLOCKED WITH SUPPLY FAN PROOF OF FLOW AND SHALL BE EQUIPPED WITH A FACTORY HIGH-LIMIT THERMAL CUTOUT.
4. THE CU SHALL BE PROTECTED BY FACTORY HIGH/LOW REFRIGERANT PRESSURE SWITCHES. A TRIP SHALL LOCK OUT THE COMPRESSOR AND REQUIRE MANUAL RESET.
5. A DIRTY FILTER CONDITION SHALL GENERATE A MAINTENANCE ALARM WITHOUT STOPPING THE UNIT.

ALARMS AND NOTIFICATIONS
1. THE BAS SHALL GENERATE ALARMS FOR ALL POINTS MARKED IN THE ALARM COLUMN OF THE DDC POINTS LIST, AND FOR THE FOLLOWING:
   A. ZONE TEMPERATURE MORE THAN 5°F (ADJ.) ABOVE OR BELOW THE ACTIVE SETPOINT FOR 15 MINUTES (ADJ.).
   B. RETURN AIR HUMIDITY ABOVE 65% RH (ADJ.) FOR 60 MINUTES (ADJ.).
   C. OUTSIDE AIRFLOW MORE THAN 20% (ADJ.) BELOW THE DESIGN MINIMUM FOR 15 MINUTES (ADJ.) WHILE THE SUPPLY FAN IS RUNNING.
   D. EMERGENCY SHUTDOWN DAMPER NOT CLOSED WITHIN 60 SECONDS (ADJ.) OF ESDS ACTIVATION, OR NOT OPEN WITH ESDS RESET.
   E. SENSOR FAILURE (ZONE TEMPERATURE, RETURN AIR HUMIDITY, OR DISCHARGE AIR TEMPERATURE).
2. ALL ALARMS SHALL BE ANNUNCIATED AT THE BAS FRONT END AND SHALL BE TIME-STAMPED AND LOGGED.
